Waiting for the camera

Your own little time machine.

  1. Choose your scene. The two AI frontal scenes are designed for guests facing the camera directly. The three original Misrah designs remain available for comparison.
  2. Open the live camera. The outline follows your face anywhere in the camera view. The booth crops and fits it automatically; keep your whole face visible, clear and steady. Auto capture takes the portrait after a suitable pose stays steady for 1.4 seconds. Capture now is a manual override.
  3. Make it yours. Automatic tone gently adjusts exposure and colour balance. Size, rotation, brightness and warmth remain adjustable. Save a PNG or JPEG at the frame’s original resolution.

Choose a straight-on AI test face to insert it immediately. Test live guidance with a sample lets you select a separate generated guest or the scene’s original face, plus dark, blurred, moving and multiple-face conditions. It uses the actual local detector and capture policy, without a webcam.

This MVP aligns a real face to a prepared opening. Large head turns and strong shadows require a better capture. Automatic tone is bounded colour and exposure correction; it does not synthesize a new light direction or hidden facial detail.

Quality thresholds are starting values for event calibration. Camera permissions, webcam autofocus, and live checks should be tested on the event computer. Everything runs locally; no internet connection or account is required. Use Chrome or Edge.
