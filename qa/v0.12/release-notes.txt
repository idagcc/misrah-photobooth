DNP issue:
The prior Windows check only allowed a difference of 0.08 inch from exactly 4 x 6. The DNP RX1 driver documents a 156.1 x 105.0 mm print area for (6x4), roughly 6.15 x 4.13 inches. Both the paper list and print submission now use the same check, with a 0.18-inch tolerance for DNP drivers and the original tolerance for other drivers. This resolves a concrete software cause of the reported size rejection; the user's actual printer was not connected here.
DNP-authored driver manual: https://www.ansystem.com/GlobDownloads/DNP/Drivers/RX1_RX1HS/RX1_RX1HS_Driver_V1.23_Instruction_Win_11_En.pdf

After connecting on Windows, open F2 > Devices. Choose Direct to selected printer, select the DNP queue, choose the single-photo (6x4) or PR (4x6) size and save.
